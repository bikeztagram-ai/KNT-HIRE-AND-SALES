# KNT quality lessons

- Preserve `main` as a clean release line; development belongs on isolated branches.
- Do not confuse successful code generation with successful product completion.
- Verify behaviour against acceptance criteria, not only build output.
- Keep business requirements and proposed ideas explicitly separated.
- Never expose credentials in source control.
- Preserve original customer evidence; AI extraction must never silently replace it.
- Shared operational records must be cloud-backed; local-only state is not sufficient.
- Invoice generation stops at KNT human review unless an explicit approval flow is added.
- Do not fake OCR/AI capabilities; label foundations honestly until the real capability exists.
- Large batches should be coherent vertical slices and repair verification failures before moving forward.
