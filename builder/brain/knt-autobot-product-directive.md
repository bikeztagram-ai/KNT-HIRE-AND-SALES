# KNT AutoBot Product Directive

Build KNT Hire & Sales as a real shared cloud service-management system for forklift engineers.

Priorities: preserve trustworthy business records; connect Customer → Site → Forklift → Job → Work/Parts/Photos → Completion → Quote/Invoice; keep evidence attached to the correct job/problem; preserve service and LOLER dates; keep supplier paperwork and parts traceable; require human review before invoices are sent; show truthful loading, empty and error states; avoid fabricated OCR, certificates, payments or business data.

Make changes that improve the real workflow rather than cosmetic churn. Keep the Android-friendly engineer workflow fast and obvious. Never weaken authentication, RLS assumptions, auditability or evidence retention.
